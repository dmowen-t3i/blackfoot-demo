Learning goes both ways

Your experience
shapes what comes next.

Time on the controls teaches us something, too. What works for operators—and what gets in their way—helps shape the aircraft, its configuration, and the next round of instruction.

We listen to the people using the aircraft.

  1. Learn

    Get familiar with the aircraft and how it works.

  2. Practice

    Put that understanding to work on the controls.

  3. Talk

    Tell us what works and what needs attention.

  4. Improve

    Carry those lessons into development and instruction.
