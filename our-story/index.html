Meet the people
behind T3i.

WHAT T3i STANDS FOR

Teamwork. Integrity.
Initiative. Innovation.

These values guide the people behind Blackfoot: working alongside your team, being clear about what we can deliver, taking ownership, and improving the aircraft through experience.

OUR BEGINNING / 2014

Built to close
a support gap.

Shane Reilly’s experience of inadequate instructor support during his military career helped inspire T3i. Founded in 2014, the company put the customer’s mission at the center of its work.

EMPLOYEE OWNED + OPERATED

A team of owners.

Employee ownership and annual profit-sharing put our people at the center of the company. The people doing the work share in what they build.
